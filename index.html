Cruxal helps manufacturing leaders make better decisions about important new-product programs as the evidence behind their business case changes.

Important programs rarely fail all at once.

They weaken over time.

Customer expectations change. Technical assumptions prove stronger or weaker than expected. Costs move. Timelines shift. Capacity tightens. Competitive conditions change.

The information usually exists somewhere: across business cases, gate reviews, account plans, spreadsheets, systems, meetings, and people’s judgment.

What is often missing is a continuously updated view of whether the logic behind the original commitment still holds.

By the time the consequences are obvious, the best options may already be gone.

Cruxal keeps the decision logic live.

Cruxal turns the assumptions, evidence, dependencies, economics, and decision conditions behind an important program into a maintained decision model.

Model what must be true.

Make the critical assumptions and dependencies behind the program explicit, including important conditions that may never have been clearly stated.

Keep it connected to evidence.

As customer, technical, commercial, operational, and financial evidence changes, Cruxal reassesses the case rather than waiting for lagging results.

Show what the change means.

Cruxal connects new evidence to the consequences for the program and business case, and helps management understand what choices still remain while there is time to act.

Starting with customer-driven new product programs.

Cruxal is initially focused on consequential decisions about live customer-driven NPD programs in manufacturing, where commercial, technical, operational, and financial assumptions are tightly connected and the cost of learning too late can be significant.
